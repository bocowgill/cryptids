# Scaling decision protocol after trial 96

Trial 96 is a screening comparison, not a scalability result. It must finish
the source adapter, actual certificates, corruption tests and all private audits
before any timings justify expanding the route. No full-machine saving follows
from it. Accepted machine: 769,344 states; proposed machine: 310,029 states.

## Substantial coverage

If the screening result supports continuing, use the same numeric recipe on
the original at-least-1/16 and at-least-1/4 source prefixes. Preserve the exact
source collection, ordered coverage theorem, actual node/return census and
independently checked regression block. Inherit earlier certificates rather
than repeat already checked prefixes. Check every bit and store, not a random
subset of transitions. Do not rebuild observation caches.

Keep the original 180/210-second tier budgets, including audits, and the overall
1,200-second process-group-plus-driver budget. Record any effective reduction
of a tier's budget caused by earlier setup. Such a truncated trial is not a
test of the full nominal tier budget.

After both complete, assess larger prefixes and full source coverage. A positive
decision requires increasing substantial portions, not just the three screening
operations. Track source labels, original paid states, both-bit rows, checking,
assembly and audit costs separately. Later full-machine linking and final review
remain separate obligations even if all source rows pass.

## Cost and uncertainty

Report fixed preparation and replay cost separately from new source checking,
literal loading, collection assembly, export and private audit. Measure peak
memory for each stage. Use same-run marginal costs between completed tiers as
the primary throughput evidence. Include the full audited stage, not only its
last progress marker. Record host variation when comparing different runs.

Only after substantial tiers pass, estimate full cost from both paid-state and
source-node/return coverage. Report a range using the observed rates, remaining
source mix, larger-module assembly and dependency-audit growth. A projection is
not a runtime bound. If no completed tier supports extrapolation, state that a
credible full-machine estimate is unavailable.

## Continue, redesign or abandon

- Continue when total cost falls materially and increasing completed tiers
  support a credible path through every original row and full-machine linking.
- Redesign if source fidelity reproduces the old normalization cost, setup
  dominates despite lower checker time, or memory/time grows faster than the
  completed coverage can justify. Identify which repeated category to eliminate
  before another trial; a source-error repair alone is not scaling evidence.
- Abandon a representation when bounded evidence shows that its overhead cannot
  plausibly finish the complete certificate. Preserve its verified lemmas and
  failure packet for reuse; do not repeat it with a larger cap by default.

The original 6/10 GiB experiment is complete: extra memory delayed failure and
completed zero row modules. No 12 GiB experiment or paid runner is planned.
Frozen specification, original obligations, remote-only computation, genuine
failure reporting and outreach hold stay in force at every decision.
