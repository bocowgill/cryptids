## New merged work and its applicability

### PR 9: parametric proofs for whole tape passes

[PR 9](https://github.com/google-deepmind/cryptids/pull/9) was merged October 20.
The most relevant example is
[WilyCoyote.lean](https://github.com/google-deepmind/cryptids/blob/6c28d8524fa008c6fb5aadb759df74a55ce96423/BusyBeaver/BB33/WilyCoyote.lean).

Source anchors: `StepSpec` at line 111, `wilyPass_reachesPlus` at 187,
`wilyPass_halts` at 280, and `StepSpec.is_valid_acceleration` at 359.
Two machine modules provide their local transition obligations and reuse the
same induction over arbitrary tape words. The proof describes a routine's
behavior structurally instead of unfolding every execution independently.

**Transferable move:** use a parametric routine specification plus local
transition obligations when certifying repeated chess interpreter routines or
formulaic state sharing. This could help replace large concrete certificates.
The same-node store-sharing diagnostic is 339,898 states within the existing
459,315 opportunity, but is not an independently valid or additive reduction.
Successor closure still has to be proved for the actual proposed partition.

**Mismatch:** this implementation concerns three symbols and three states,
uses upstream's changed tape representation, and proves macro execution.
It is not a binary chess quotient checker or a remedy for RowTree cache validity.
Reusing its proof architecture requires a new bridge in our existing semantics.
No proof-time saving or extra state saving has been measured here.

### PRs 12 and 13: compose accelerations and collapse repeated drains

[PR 12](https://github.com/google-deepmind/cryptids/pull/12) was merged October 22;
[PR 13](https://github.com/google-deepmind/cryptids/pull/13) October 23.

In upstream `BusyBeaver/Basic.lean`, `IsValidAcceleration.collatzHalts_iff`
at line 928 relates two valid accelerations of one underlying machine.
`CollatzStepsTo` at 949 and `IsValidAcceleration.of_collatz` at 1090 compose
a higher-level transition with one or more lower-level transitions.
PR 13 applies this infrastructure to arithmetic drain formulas using division,
remainders, and minimums; inspect the checked source rather than relying on
the PR title's machine count, which differs from parts of its updated body.

**Transferable move:** express a tape scan, arithmetic loop, or initializer
routine at its boundary configurations and prove a multi-step summary.
Such summaries may reduce the cost of bounded differential tests or of proving
a future physical optimization correct.

**Limits:** these PRs accelerate descriptions of fixed machines. Their faster
high-level transitions do not reduce the machines' physical control states.
They do not provide a compiler from arithmetic jumps to a smaller binary TM,
nor a shortcut for concrete quotient row or cache proofs. Positive progress
of simulated steps and exact halting cases remain essential; stuttering alone
does not establish both halting directions. Full quadratic chess startup
simulation remains outside the campaign's allowed approach.

The PR 13 review found parameter-offset, boundary, and documentation mismatches
that were subsequently repaired. A transplanted formula should have explicit
zero, threshold, parity, and saturated-subtraction cases plus source checks.
