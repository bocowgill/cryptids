# Gates: authorized decoder-sharing pilot

OWNS: scripts/riemann_share/**, lakefile.riemann-share.toml,
  .github/workflows/riemann_share.yml, research/riemann-reduction/**

One solo execution leaf: prepare the fixed family, run one free remote job,
retain its exact outcome, then preserve the hold. No local executable mathematics.
The original proposal remains unchanged as the approved contract.

- [ ] D1: The remote producer implements exactly the approved 30 aliases and caps.
  EVIDENCE: Pending source review, pinned baseline comparison and launch provenance.
- [ ] D2: Exactly one free job runs with no retry and records actual resource use.
  EVIDENCE: Pending public-runner metadata, run attempt and resource summary.
- [ ] D3: Every fixed alias receives a reproducible typed-word disposition.
  EVIDENCE: Pending remote per-choice data and independent replay.
- [ ] D4: The job returns certified counterexamples, a complete RH certificate,
  or the earliest exact obstruction with unexecuted obligations explicit.
  EVIDENCE: Pending remote Lean build, full-private audit and rejection controls,
    or retained evidence identifying the earliest failed stage or open lemma.
- [ ] D5: Evidence is retained and baseline, historical proof evidence and Chess
  pause are preserved; broader search remains held.
  EVIDENCE: Pending artifact retention, exact baseline/pause comparisons and final review.

These are manual evidence-integration gates: each reviews external job artifacts
and scope, rather than running the mathematics locally. No met gate implies an
RH saving unless the full public theorem and all certification checks pass.
